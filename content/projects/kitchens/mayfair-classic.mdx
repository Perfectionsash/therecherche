A complete reimagining of a Mayfair townhouse kitchen, blending timeless shaker proportions with contemporary functionality. The brief called for a kitchen that felt as though it had always been part of the house — respecting the Georgian architecture while serving a modern family's needs.

## Design Approach

We worked closely with the interior designer to develop a palette that would honour the property's heritage. The hand-painted finish in Farrow & Ball's 'Railings' provides a deep, sophisticated blue-black that changes beautifully through the day. Brass cup handles and knobs add warmth and tactility.

## Key Features

**Walk-in Larder** — A dedicated pantry space with adjustable shelving, pull-out drawers, and a cooling drawer for wine.

**Central Island** — The Calacatta Viola marble worktop with its dramatic veining becomes the room's focal point. The island houses a prep sink, induction hob, and seating for four.

**Appliance Integration** — Miele column fridge/freezer, steam oven, and coffee machine sit behind matching cabinet fronts. A Sub-Zero wine column flanks the larder entrance.

## Client Feedback

> "The team understood exactly what we wanted — a kitchen that feels like it's always been part of the house, but works brilliantly for how we actually live. The larder is a game-changer."
