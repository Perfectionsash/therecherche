A light-filled kitchen in a Hampstead mews house, designed around a love of cooking and entertaining. The handleless oak cabinetry creates a seamless, almost furniture-like quality, while the Gaggenau appliances provide professional-grade performance.

## Design Approach

The clients wanted a kitchen that disappeared into the architecture — more living room than utility space. Natural oak veneer with a matte lacquer finish brings warmth without visual weight. The recessed channel hardware in black anodised aluminium is almost invisible until you need it.

## Key Features

**Waterfall Island** — The Dekton 'Trilium' worktop wraps down the sides of the island, concealing an integrated downdraft extractor that rises only when needed.

**Hidden Appliances** — Gaggenau's 400 Series fits entirely behind pocket doors: oven, steam oven, coffee machine, and warming drawer all accessible but out of sight.

**Scullery Access** — A pocket door leads to a compact scullery with second sink, dishwasher, and storage for small appliances.
