Floor-to-ceiling sliding wardrobes in a Notting Hill Victorian conversion. The textured glass and bronze mirror panels reflect light through the room while concealing a highly organised storage system.

## Design Approach

The brief was for maximum storage with minimum visual impact in a bedroom with limited wall space. Four sliding panels on a concealed track system provide full access without swing clearance. The bronze mirror panels add depth and warmth; the textured glass diffuses light beautifully.

## Key Features

**Concealed Track** — The track is recessed into the ceiling, completely invisible when doors are closed. Soft-close mechanism ensures silent operation.

**Modular Internals** — The aluminium frame system allows infinite reconfiguration: hanging rails, shelves, drawers, and trouser racks all adjust without tools.

**Perimeter Lighting** — LED strips run the full perimeter of each compartment, controlled via remote with dimming and colour temperature presets.
