A generous walk-in wardrobe created from a former bedroom in a Chelsea townhouse. The American walnut cabinetry provides rich warmth, while the satin lacquer protects against daily wear.

## Design Approach

The client wanted a dressing room that felt luxurious but entirely practical. Every item has a designated home — from the angled shoe shelves (holding 60+ pairs) to the velvet-lined jewellery drawers and dedicated tie/belt racks.

## Key Features

**Central Island** — A freestanding island with leather-wrapped top houses drawers for accessories, a pull-out mirror, and a charging station for devices.

**Integrated Lighting** — LED profiles run within every shelf and rail, triggered by motion sensors. Colour temperature shifts from cool (morning) to warm (evening).

**Dedicated Zones** — Separate his/hers hanging sections, shared folded storage, seasonal rotation at high level, and a shoe wall visible from the entrance.

## Client Feedback

> "Getting dressed used to be a chore — now it's the best part of the day. The lighting alone makes everything look better."
