A garden-level apartment in Kensington required a kitchen that maximised light and space while delivering serious cooking performance. The clients are enthusiastic home cooks who wanted professional-grade appliances in a clean, architectural aesthetic.

**The Brief**
- Handleless, minimalist aesthetic
- Professional cooking appliances (Gaggenau)
- Integrated downdraft extraction
- Seamless porcelain worktops
- Hidden butler's pantry for prep and storage

**The Solution**
The kitchen runs along one long wall with a central island. Matte anthracite lacquer cabinets feature an integrated aluminium J-profile for finger-pull opening — no handles, no visual interruption. Push-to-open mechanisms throughout.

The island houses a Gaggenau 400 Series induction hob with flush-mounted downdraft extraction that rises only when needed. The Dekton 'Kelya' porcelain worktop continues up the wall as a full-height splashback, creating a single material plane.

A Gaggenau 200 Series oven, combi-steam oven, and warming drawer sit in a tall bank. A Liebherr wine cabinet and integrated fridge-freezer complete the run. Behind a pocket door, a butler's pantry provides a second sink, dishwasher, and open shelving for prep — keeping the main kitchen immaculate during dinner parties.

**Client Feedback**
> "It's the kitchen we've always wanted — beautiful to look at, but built for serious cooking. The downdraft extraction is brilliant; you'd never know it's there until you need it."
> — Michael & Emma, Kensington
