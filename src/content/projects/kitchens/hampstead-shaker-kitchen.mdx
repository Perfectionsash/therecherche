A Victorian terrace in Hampstead needed a kitchen that respected the home's heritage while functioning for modern family life. The clients wanted a traditional shaker aesthetic but with contemporary usability — deep drawers, integrated appliances, and a layout that flows.

**The Brief**
- Replace a fragmented 1980s kitchen-diner
- Create a sociable cooking space open to the garden
- Hand-painted finish in a heritage colour
- Walk-in pantry for dry goods and small appliances
- Seating at the island for casual dining

**The Solution**
We designed a symmetrical shaker kitchen centred on a substantial island. The cabinetry is hand-painted in Farrow & Ball 'Railings' — a deep blue-black that reads as neutral in daylight but reveals its colour at night. Unlacquered brass knobs and pulls will patina gracefully.

The island houses the sink and dishwasher, keeping cleanup social. A honed Carrara marble worktop runs throughout, including the island's 300mm overhang for seating. The appliance wall integrates a Gaggenau oven, Miele steam oven, and Sub-Zero fridge-freezer behind shaker panels.

A separate walk-in pantry behind a pocket door provides 3m of shelving, deep drawers for root vegetables, and a dedicated coffee station with plumbed-in Miele machine.
