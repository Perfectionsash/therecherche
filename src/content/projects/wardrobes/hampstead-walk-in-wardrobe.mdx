A master bedroom in a Hampstead villa had an unused dressing room that the clients wanted to transform into a proper walk-in wardrobe. They needed space for an extensive clothing collection, plus dedicated storage for shoes, handbags, and jewellery.

**The Brief**
- Maximise hanging space for dresses, coats, and suits
- Dedicated shoe storage for 50+ pairs
- Jewellery and watch storage with velvet lining
- Central island for packing and accessory organisation
- Integrated lighting that activates on entry
- Hand-painted finish to match bedroom decor

**The Solution**
The 4.2m x 3.1m room is configured with hanging on three walls — double hanging on the long walls for shirts and trousers, single hanging with pull-down rail on the short wall for dresses and coats. The fourth wall houses floor-to-ceiling shelving with angled shoe shelves (52 pairs capacity) and pull-out handbag shelves.

The central island is the focal point: honed marble top with recessed leather-lined jewellery trays, three deep drawers with custom dividers for watches and cufflinks, and a pull-out valet rod. All drawers are leather-lined oak with brass-fronted pulls.

Lighting is fully integrated: linear LED in the pelmet above hanging rails, under-shelf strips on shoe shelves, and motion-activated strips in the island drawers. Everything switches on automatically when the door opens.

The finish is Farrow & Ball 'Slipper Satin' — a warm, soft grey-green that complements the bedroom's scheme. Brushed brass hardware throughout.
