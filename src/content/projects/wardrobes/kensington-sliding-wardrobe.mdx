A bedroom in a Kensington mews house had a challenging alcove that the clients wanted to convert into a full-width wardrobe. The narrow room meant swinging doors would impede circulation, making sliding doors the only practical choice.

**The Brief**
- Full-width wardrobe in 3.6m alcove
- Sliding doors to save space
- Mirror panels to visually expand the room
- Custom internal layout for two people
- Soft-close everything

**The Solution**
Three-panel sliding doors: centre panel in bronze mirror, outer panels in painted glass (Farrow & Ball 'Off-Black'). The mirror reflects light from the window, visually doubling the room's depth. The painted glass adds depth and conceals the wardrobe contents.

Internally, the left section has double hanging with a pull-down rail for the upper section. The centre has adjustable shelving with two soft-close drawers at waist height. The right section has single hanging for dresses/coats plus a pull-out trouser rack and tie rack.

The track is fully concealed in the ceiling pelmet with integrated LED lighting that activates when the doors open. Flush pull handles in brushed brass. Soft-close dampers on all three doors.

**Client Feedback**
> "The bronze mirror is extraordinary — it makes the bedroom feel twice as big. And the sliding action is so smooth you can open it with one finger."
> — Richard & Lisa, Kensington
